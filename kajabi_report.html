Cofounder introduces a crack. When AI plans the product, writes the copy, and sets the price, the mirror reflects something the expert didn't put there. What This Signals

The creator economy is bifurcating — not between AI users and resisters, but between creators whose value comes from volume (AI makes them faster) and those whose value comes from trust (AI makes them suspect).

The first major creator platform to offer a "verified expert" credential — structural proof that content and coaching are human-originated — will own the premium tier. The same dynamic that created "organic" in food is arriving in digital knowledge. Your About page already owns the thesis. Cofounder currently undermines it.

TrendLoom estimate: 8–14 months before "verified human content" becomes a recognized market differentiator.

The Opening

Reframing Cofounder not as "AI that thinks for you" but as "AI that proves you did the thinking" — with visible attribution of which ideas are the expert's and which were AI-assisted — would make you the first creator platform to turn the authenticity crisis into a trust credential.
