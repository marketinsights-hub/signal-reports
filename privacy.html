Privacy Policy

Market Insights Hub by TrendLoom
Last updated: February 2025

This privacy policy applies exclusively to the research reports published on the Market Insights Hub, hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not cover the main TrendLoom website, which has its own separate privacy policy.

What We Collect

When you visit a report page, we may collect the following information: which report you viewed, the date and time of your visit, and how long you spent on the page. If you arrived via a personalized link we sent you (for example, through LinkedIn), we can associate your visit with your identity based on a unique identifier in that link. If you arrived via a direct link or found the page on your own, we do not identify you.

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics tools on these report pages.

Why We Collect It

We use this data to understand whether our research content is relevant and useful to the people we share it with. This helps us improve our reports and tailor our communication. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in evaluating engagement with our content (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

How Long We Keep It

We retain visit data for up to 12 months, after which it is deleted.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any data we hold about you. You also have the right to object to our processing of your data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.
